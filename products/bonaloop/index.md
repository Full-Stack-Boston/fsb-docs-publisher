Private admin/eng/ops runbooks live on the lab Wiki under `Products/Bonaloop/` (Authentik). They are not mirrored here.

- Product overview and host map
- Museum / showcase links (php, splash) that are scored public
- Pointers to App Store / marketing URLs on [bonaloop.com](https://bonaloop.com)

## What is not here

- Outbound Live controls, vault handles, staff impersonation, ban playbooks
- Internal QC dispute procedures
